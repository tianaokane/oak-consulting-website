Packages & pricing

Support designed to match your capacity and budget.

Some organisations need a light-touch steer. Others want a long-term partner. Our packages are clear, flexible and grounded in what's realistic — and every engagement starts with a free conversation.

Which package?

Choosing the right level of support.

The right package depends on how much internal capacity you have, how many projects you're running and how ambitious your funding targets are. If none of these feels quite right, we can discuss a tailored arrangement.

  • Choose Basic if you mainly need to stay informed about funding opportunities and want occasional bid-writing support on demand.
  • Choose Standard if you expect to pursue several grants or tenders each year and want ongoing, joined-up support.
  • Choose Premium if you want Oak Consultancy as a close, long-term partner involved in board reporting and a higher volume of bids.

Billing & transparency

How pricing works.

  • All packages begin with a free initial conversation — no obligation.
  • Packages are usually agreed for 12 months to allow time for planning and delivery.
  • Hours are tracked and reported so you can see exactly how support is being used.
  • Any additional work outside your package is agreed in advance at a clear hourly rate.
  • We're always happy to discuss budget constraints and find a realistic arrangement.

Not sure which option fits?

The best next step is a short, free conversation. We'll listen to where you are and suggest something realistic — no hard sell, no obligation.
