About us

Calm, practical support for organisations rooted in their communities.

We help charities, community groups, schools and sports clubs make sense of funding — plainly, honestly and without pressure. If funding feels like a maze, we're the people you can talk it through with.

Our story

We started Oak because we kept seeing the same problem.

Organisation after organisation — delivering brilliant work in their communities — stretched thin. Staff and volunteers juggling front-line delivery, governance, safeguarding, facilities, reporting and everything in between. Funding applications squeezed into evenings and weekends.

Oak Consultancy was set up to offer a different kind of support: practical, honest and tailored to the realities of smaller organisations — not written assuming there's a dedicated fundraising team.

How we work

The things people notice most when they work with us.

Plain language

We explain processes and funding requirements in clear terms, so you always know what's happening and can explain it to your board or committee.

Honest advice

If something isn't realistic, or a fund isn't a good fit, we'll say so. We'd rather protect your time and energy than chase every opportunity on paper.

Realistic plans

We work with the time and capacity you actually have. Plans are designed to be lived with, not just presented once and then forgotten.

Long-term thinking

We help you build a pipeline, not just chase one-off pots. The aim is to give your organisation a steadier footing over time.

Partnership

Your knowledge of your community combined with our experience of funding and planning is what makes the work effective. We build from there, together.

Integrity

We only encourage bids and tenders that feel appropriate and realistic for your organisation — even when that means advising against an opportunity.
