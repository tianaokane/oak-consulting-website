How it works

Straightforward from the first conversation.

We keep the process as light on your time as possible. Here's what working with Oak looks like.

1

Initial conversation

We talk through your organisation, current work, upcoming deadlines and what you'd like to achieve.

2

Agree focus and priorities

Together we decide what to tackle first, and whether a one-off project or ongoing package makes most sense.

3

We do the work alongside you

We draft, shape and strengthen bids, plans and tenders — keeping your voice at the centre.

4

Submit and learn

We support submission and use any feedback to make the next round simpler and more successful.
